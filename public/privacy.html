Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

At JSON Formatter & Validator, available at our website, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information and why our tool is safe for processing your data.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing (Zero Data Transmission)

All JSON formatting, beautifying, parsing, minifying, and syntax validation processes take place entirely inside your personal web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not send, transmit, store, or log any JSON data, text, API keys, credentials, or payload snippets to any external server or database. Your data never leaves your device.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not require user accounts, registration, email addresses, or subscriptions to access our services. You can use all features freely and anonymously without providing any personal information.

3. Standard Server Logs

Like most standard websites, standard cloud hosting infrastructure may automatically log basic, non-personally identifiable technical information (such as browser type, referring/exit pages, date/time stamps, and IP addresses) solely for maintaining server reliability, uptime, and security. These logs contain no user-entered JSON content or personal identification.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

JSON Formatter & Validator does not use tracking cookies or persistent advertising identifiers. Any user interface preferences (such as indentation style) are stored purely in transient client state.

5. Security Recommendations

While our application operates strictly locally in your browser and never transmits your data to our servers, we always recommend adhering to standard security best practices by removing highly confidential passwords or production secrets before pasting into any browser tool.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional improvements or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
